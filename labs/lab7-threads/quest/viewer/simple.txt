# One cube and one sphere: the scene language at its smallest, and a render in well under a
# second. The light of radius 0.8 gives soft shadows in part 4, its colour above 1 the need

# with the same colour twice.

plane     y 0             checker 0.62 0.64 0.67 / 0.62 0.64 0.67
