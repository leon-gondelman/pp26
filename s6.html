Session 6

Asynchronous Programming

concurrency on one thread, from the ground up: continuations, promises, the event loop

Part 2 opens: coordination and communication, what we require of a concurrent program, and the four sessions on one bank; then concurrency on one thread, where a function that waits gives the thread back.

Lecture

Main topics
The introduction to Part 2: Dijkstra's bounded buffer, safety and liveness, the four sessions on one system. Then asynchronous programming: suspension points, waiting is not running, run-to-completion, atomicity between suspension points, the race on one thread.
